About the Canom X200
The Canom X200 is a compact mirrorless camera built for everyday storytelling. Light enough to carry all day and powerful enough to handle low light, fast action, and sweeping landscapes, it helps you capture sharp, vivid photos and smooth 4K video without a complicated setup. Whether you are documenting events, travelling, or exploring the city after dark, it is ready when you are.
